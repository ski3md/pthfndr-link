Getting started

What you need

  • A Mac with macOS 14 or later, with P@THFNDR Link installed.
  • An Android phone (Android 12 or later) with the P@THFNDR Link companion app.
  • Both devices on the same Wi-Fi network for pairing.

Pair your phone

  1. On the Mac, open P@THFNDR Link and choose Devices → Pair a phone. A QR code appears.
  2. On the phone, open P@THFNDR Link, tap Scan and scan the QR code, then tap Start the secure link.
  3. Check that the verification code matches on both screens and confirm it on the phone. Allow the phone on the Mac when asked.
  4. On the phone, allow notification access for P@THFNDR Link when Settings opens.

The status then reads Connected on both devices.

Troubleshooting

The status says “Disconnected” or “Connecting”

  • Make sure the phone and the Mac are on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks and some workplace networks block devices from seeing each other.
  • On the Mac, allow local network access: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, turn on P@THFNDR Link.
  • Keep P@THFNDR Link open on the Mac (closing the window is fine; quitting stops the connection).
  • If your network hides devices from each other, open Connection help in the phone app and enter the address shown on the Mac under Devices.

The status says “Needs attention”

Follow the one action shown under the status (for example, confirm the verification code, or allow notification access). If the codes on the two screens do not match, choose They don't match and pair again.

Notifications don't appear on the Mac

  • On the phone: Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications (or Notification access), turn on P@THFNDR Link.
  • Exclude P@THFNDR Link from battery optimisation on the phone so Android doesn't stop it in the background.
  • On the Mac: System Settings → Notifications → P@THFNDR Link controls banners. Activity in the app still shows notifications if banners are off.
  • Apps listed under Apps kept on this phone in the phone app are not mirrored.

Reply, music or call controls are unavailable

Controls appear only when the phone offers them for that notification and the phone is Connected. Music and call controls can be switched on in the phone app under Allow from your Mac.

Start over

On the phone choose Forget this Mac… (it asks first), and on the Mac choose Devices → Remove…. Then pair again.

Contact

Questions or problems: open a support request. Please include your Mac and Android versions and what the status shows. Never include message contents or pairing codes.
