Privacy Policy

P@THFNDR Link for Mac and Android · Effective 4 October 2026
Support

Summary

We do not collect your data. P@THFNDR Link works directly between your own Android phone and your own Mac. Your notifications, messages, replies, pictures and other information are not sent to us or to any server we operate, and we have no access to them.

What the app handles, and where it stays

  • Notifications and their content (app name, title, text, pictures, media and call information) are sent from your phone to your paired Mac over your local network, encrypted, so the Mac can show them. On the Mac they are kept in memory while the app runs; small app icons and temporary copies of pictures you choose to open are stored inside the app's own storage on your Mac.
  • Replies and actions you choose on the Mac (Reply, Dismiss, Mark as read, media and call controls) are sent only to your paired phone, encrypted, which carries them out.
  • Pairing information and security keys stay on your devices: on the Mac in the Keychain; on the phone, the device keys are kept in the Android Keystore and the pairing record in the app's private storage with its secret sealed by a Keystore key. Only what is needed to set up the encrypted connection is exchanged between your two devices.
  • Diagnostics about the connection (for example, whether it is connected and counts of messages) are stored on your Mac only and contain no message contents. They are shared only if you choose to copy and send them.
  • Local network addresses are used only to connect your two devices.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or servers for your data.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Other services involved

  • Apple macOS shows notification banners through Notification Center on your Mac, under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google Play services provides the QR code scanner on Android, which runs on your phone, under Google's privacy policy.
  • If you open a link (for example, a web version of a messaging app or a music search), it opens in your browser under that website's policy.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn off notification access for P@THFNDR Link in Android settings to stop mirroring.
  • Choose Forget this Mac… on the phone and remove the phone under Devices on the Mac to end the pairing.
  • Deleting the app from either device removes the information it stored there.

Children

P@THFNDR Link is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
